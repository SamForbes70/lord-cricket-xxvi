# Lord Cricket XXVI: Studio Production Directive 05 (27 November 2026)

This records the owner's Studio Production Directive 05, "Creative Production: Professional Gaming Studio Quality" (programme name Project Pavilion: Creative Production Uplift), and the working decisions taken under it. It changes no code and marks no gate as passed. Directives 01 to 03 and the owner decisions recorded in them stay in force. No Directive 04 has been received in this repository.

## The directive, in summary

- **Objective:** move Lord Cricket XXVI from a technically sophisticated simulation to an aesthetically distinctive, emotionally engaging, highly playable and commercially competitive cricket management game.
- **Immediate objective:** a substantially improved, recognisably professional T20 match experience and Clubhouse.
- **Overall target:** at least 85/100 on the directive's 100-point Creative Production Scorecard, no category below 70% of its marks, subject to independent acceptance. A self-assigned score is not acceptance.
- **Passes:** CP01 audit and competitive benchmark; CP02 brand and design system; CP03 live match broadcast interface; CP04 match events, motion and atmosphere; CP05 club identities; CP06 player portraits and profiles; CP07 Clubhouse and career; CP08 statistical visualisation; CP09 audio; CP10 commentary; CP11 results, awards and championships; CP12 onboarding and navigation; CP13 accessibility and performance; CP14 integrated creative acceptance.
- **Statuses:** PROPOSED, DESIGNED, IMPLEMENTED, INTEGRATED, VERIFIED, HUMAN TESTED, ACCEPTED, recorded in `creative/CREATIVE_PRODUCTION_LEDGER.md`.
- **Mandatory constraints (section 16):** presentation consumes authoritative match events and never creates them; no change to outcomes or the random stream for cosmetic reasons; no presentation-side cricket state; deterministic replay, save compatibility, the unified advance (#173) and the computer captain and human boundaries are preserved; no unapproved models, runtime dependencies or network requirements; Chromium, Firefox and Windows stay supported; accessibility and reduced motion are part of each change.
- **Not authorised:** altering outcomes for entertainment, bypassing repository controls, waiving gates, unlicensed intellectual property, purchasing assets without approval, activating research models, unversioned persistence changes, invented evidence, claiming commercial acceptance without evidence.

## Working decisions under this directive

1. **Integration.** The owner's standing instruction of 27 November 2026 ("merge the next PRs once green and go with your recommendations") covers the creative passes: each pass is a draft pull request, merged only when every hosted check passes on its head and it merges cleanly, after its claims are checked against the branch.
2. **Image generation.** The only image-generation tool available in the development environment spends credits on a third-party account. That is a purchase decision the directive reserves to the owner, so it is not used without the owner's approval. Assets are original SVG, CSS or procedural work, with provenance recorded in the asset manifest. Existing portraits and art keep their recorded provenance.
3. **Audio.** Only original or appropriately licensed audio is used, with source, author, licence and intended use recorded. No broadcast recordings, commercial music or recognisable voices.
4. **Human testing.** Participants are recruited and observed by the owner (`PLAYTEST_PROTOCOL.md`). Until sessions are recorded, every pass is reported with human testing outstanding. No participants, quotations or ratings are invented.
5. **Order.** CP01 first (audit with baseline screenshots, and a sourced benchmark). Then the design system tokens (CP02) and the T20 match and Clubhouse improvements the audit ranks highest (CP03, CP07), each as a bounded, separately verifiable pull request.
6. **Scope.** No new clubs, competitions or simulation capabilities are added for presentation. Where a creative feature needs one, the dependency is recorded instead of a misleading placeholder.
