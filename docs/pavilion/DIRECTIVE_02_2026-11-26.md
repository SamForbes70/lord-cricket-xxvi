# Lord Cricket XXVI: Studio Production Directive 02 (26 November 2026)

This records the owner's Studio Production Directive 02 and where each item stands. It changes no code and marks no gate as passed. It builds on `DIRECTIVE_01_2026-11-26.md` and `DIRECTION_2026-11-25_CORRECTION.md`, which stay in force.

## The directive, in summary

- **Objective:** a commercially competitive cricket management game with authentic cricket, meaningful strategic decisions, exceptional playability and long-term player attachment.
- **Milestone:** one exceptionally enjoyable and statistically credible complete T20 season, using the existing eight-club world.
- **Restrictions:**
  - No substantial expansion of the world.
  - No unnecessary mechanics.
  - No expensive 3D animation before the T20 career milestone passes.
  - No unapproved probability model is activated.
  - Cricket outcomes are never manipulated to manufacture excitement.
  - Automated functional testing is never treated as evidence of human enjoyment.
- **Reporting, for every pass:**
  - source commit;
  - implemented functionality;
  - player benefit;
  - statistical evidence;
  - verification results;
  - outstanding defects;
  - acceptance status.

  Each feature is reported as proposed, implemented, integrated, verified, human tested or accepted.
- **Success:** players voluntarily return, become invested in their teams, recognise individual cricketers and understand the consequences of their management decisions.

## Owner decisions, 26 November 2026

The owner said "go with your recommendations" to five open questions.

1. **Match playtest (E2):**
   - two or three people who have not seen the game, one T20 match each;
   - run with `PLAYTEST_PROTOCOL.md`, recording hesitation, comprehension and whether they ask to play again;
   - the owner arranges the participants;
   - the playtest build is packaged from `main` once the decision-panel fairness fix (#160) is merged.
2. **Bowling plans (A4, B1):** plans should be able to matter, modestly. A good plan is worth a few runs an innings and is visible in the post-match analysis, without becoming the dominant lever. Effects are labelled design choices, because Cricsheet records no length or plan data. This work follows captain V5.
3. **Final independent evaluation (A5):** the held-out Cricsheet period (from 30 August 2025) is used once, after captain V5 and the bowling-plan work are merged. Any model change after it needs fresh independent evidence.
4. **Player development (C3):** a real, hidden growth rate per player, shown later as a range by scouting option A. It is built after the season-end work, under a new saved-data version that the owner approves before it is written.
5. **Gameplay release of the calibrated model (A6):** decided only after A5, with its results, compatibility checks and a recommendation. Nothing is activated without explicit owner approval.
