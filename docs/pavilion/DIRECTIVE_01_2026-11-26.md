# Lord Cricket XXVI: Studio Production Directive 01 (26 November 2026)

This records the owner's Studio Production Directive 01 and how it maps onto work already under way. It changes no code and marks no gate as passed. It builds on `DIRECTION_2026-11-25_CORRECTION.md`, which stays in force.

## The directive, in summary

- **Objective:** move the game from an advanced technical alpha to a compelling, statistically credible and commercially competitive cricket management game.
- **Milestone:** one exceptionally enjoyable, complete T20 season with the existing eight clubs. No substantial expansion of the cricket universe until this milestone passes independent human acceptance.
- **Priorities:**
  1. Complete stage C calibration: excessive dot balls, low scoring, skill differentiation, tactical effectiveness and unrealistic bowling deployment.
  2. Meaningful, measurable effects for batting intent, bowling plans, field placement, player selection, matchups and fatigue.
  3. A better match experience: anticipation, contextual commentary, atmosphere, meaningful decisions and satisfying conclusions.
  4. Deeper career engagement: distinctive player identities, scouting uncertainty, youth development, rivalries, club news and managerial progression.
  5. A coherent, professional sports broadcast and management interface.
  6. Independent match and season playtests that record observed behaviour, friction, comprehension, enjoyment and voluntary replay.
  7. Deterministic simulation, replay integrity, versioned contracts, save compatibility and comprehensive automated verification.
- **Allocation:** cricket intelligence 40%, entertainment and player experience 40%, engineering quality and integration 20%.
- **Engineering constraints:**
  - Match processing stays authoritative and event driven.
  - No manufactured excitement, artificial close finishes or results changed for narrative convenience.
  - No experimental probability model is activated without statistical validation and explicit release approval.
- **Reporting, for every development pass:**
  - previous and current source commits;
  - improvements implemented;
  - player benefit;
  - before and after measurements;
  - automated verification results;
  - human playtest evidence, where available;
  - outstanding defects and limitations;
  - proposed next action.

  Each item is reported as proposed, implemented, integrated, verified, human tested or accepted. Enjoyment scores, test results and statistical validation are never invented.
- **Definition of success:** a player voluntarily plays another match, becomes attached to individual cricketers, understands why management decisions matter, and finishes a season wanting to begin another.
