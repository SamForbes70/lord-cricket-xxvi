# ADR-029: M7G empirical scoring components stay research-only

**Status:** Accepted for offline research, REJECTED for gameplay model promotion.

**Context:** M7F estimated legal ball-envelope distributions from genuine Cricsheet data, matching average T20 scoring but underestimating between-innings variability and leaving bat runs, extras and wicket party unresolved. Directly replacing the accepted `synthetic-alpha-01` probability pack would have broken deterministic history and overstated model evidence.

**Decision:** Fit a separately fingerprinted, match-level latent scoring-regime mixture from the existing chronological training cohort, exposing a fully joint *observed* category of legality, bat runs, wides, no-balls, byes, leg-byes, boundaries, wicket class and dismissed striker/non-striker. For held-out probability evaluation, marginalise regimes sequentially from the prior deliveries without using future scores. Quantise every regime/phase/wicket-band category row to 2^32 integer mass and retain an independent research RNG domain. The TypeScript adapter may translate observed categories to strictly valid `DeliveryResolution` records with explicitly synthetic, unobserved fielding placeholders for scorer testing only. It SHALL NOT be wired into production `generateDelivery()` or live match commands.

**Evidence:** On the frozen 652-match chronological holdout, log-loss improves against matched phase-only scoring components (candidate-minus-baseline -0.01283 nats/attempt, 95% match-bootstrap [-0.01505,-0.01054]). In 600 fixed-seed generated innings all seven aggregate score tolerance checks pass. All 59 supported categories and eight full research innings reconcile through unchanged scoring functions. Rare unsupported real events (36 across cohorts), two unseen heldout scoring categories, unidentifiable fielding and uncalibrated player/tactical effects prohibit gameplay promotion.

**Consequences:** Original scoring rules, SHA256_COUNTER_V1, production `synthetic-alpha-01`, generator vectors, saved games, event hashes and ODI/red-ball profiles are unaffected. M7H must independently predeclare complete legal support, fit identifiable skill effects and separately validate interventions, and run full-match/browser/replay acceptance before promoting any new versioned gameplay model. Dataset attribution: Cricsheet, ODC-By 1.0; users must provide the real archive separately.
